3D anatomy model files in this directory (public/anatomy/)
===========================================================

The .glb files and structures.json in this directory are derived from:

  BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science,
  licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan.
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html
  Source: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/bodyparts3d/LATEST/ (version 4.0)

Citation:
  Mitsuhashi N, Fujieda K, Tamura T, Kawamoto S, Takagi T, Okubo K.
  BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts.
  Nucleic Acids Res. 2009;37(Database issue):D782-5. doi:10.1093/nar/gkn613

Changes made (by scripts/anatomy/ in this repository):
  - Converted from Wavefront OBJ to glTF binary (GLB), millimetres to metres, Z-up to Y-up.
  - Grouped into render layers; a reduced-detail copy (about 25% of triangles) was made.
  - Quantised and compressed with meshopt.
  - Three source left/right labels were corrected (see data/anatomy/laterality_corrections.json).
